ℹ️ About Application

This exam paper creator application is a specialized platform designed to simplify the entire question paper preparation workflow for teachers and institutions. This app is created by Ismail Tharfeed, and this is a contact number +919483061904. It allows educators to generate standardized question papers either by scanning existing question banks or directly extracting conceptual questions from textbook lesson reading pages. The app automatically calculates blueprint marks, ensures clean layout formatting, and preserves question history securely on your device. Thank you for utilizing this application to streamline school and college examination management.
